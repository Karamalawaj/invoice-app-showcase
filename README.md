# Invoice & Operations Management System

Portfolio showcase for a private internal business application focused on invoice generation and operational workflows.

## Overview
The system supports practical day-to-day business operations around customer records, invoice creation, calculations, archiving, reporting, and workflow assistance.

## Key Capabilities
- Customer and service records
- Invoice generation and archiving
- VAT and discount calculations
- Excel-based reporting workflows
- Translation-assisted service workflows
- Internal operational management

## Tech Stack
Python · Flask · SQLite · JavaScript · Bootstrap · openpyxl

## Engineering Focus
The project emphasizes reliable business workflows, structured records, reporting, maintainable operations tooling, and practical internal usability.

## Project Status
Private internal system / portfolio showcase.
